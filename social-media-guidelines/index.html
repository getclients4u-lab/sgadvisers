Guidelines

Social Media

Guidance for firm communication and responsible online conduct.

Purpose

These guidelines are designed to help employees navigate social media responsibly while protecting client confidentiality and the firm’s reputation.

Principles

  • Protect client confidentiality and material non-public information.
  • Do not represent the firm without authorization.
  • Be respectful, accurate, and professional in all online interactions.
  • Separate personal views from firm-related commentary where appropriate.

Resources

For questions about these guidelines, contact the firm’s compliance or communications teams through the official channels listed on the Contact page.
