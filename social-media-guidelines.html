Social Media Guidelines

Please refer to the following guidelines when interacting with SGA via social media pages.

Terms of Service

Social Media Terms of Service

  • Be civil and respectful. Do not post offensive or inappropriate material.
  • Keep information private. Do not post confidential information.
  • SGA cannot respond to inquiries through social media. Use customer service information.
  • Do not post specific investment recommendations, advice, or testimonials.
  • Do not post advertisements or content meant to influence web traffic or rankings.
Disclosures

Important Disclosures

Information and opinions posted by SGA on social media are intended for informational purposes only and are not investment advice or recommendations to sell or purchase any security. Content posted on behalf of any SGA employee represents that employee’s opinion and does not represent the views of SGA.

Please consult with a qualified professional for investment advice. Content on our social media pages does not constitute an offer to sell or purchase securities.

Warning

Warning Around Company Imposters

The investment management industry, including SGA, has seen an increase in reports of fake websites and social media accounts impersonating firms and staff. These fraudsters use firm brands or names of representatives to try to scam and defraud others.

The fraudsters impersonating SGA are not authorized by SGA and SGA is not associated with accounts on social media and similar applications that request direct investments. SGA does not offer direct investment opportunities to individuals through any social media platform.

Please contact us immediately if you suspect someone is impersonating our firm.
