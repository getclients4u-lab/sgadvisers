About Us

Who We Are

Sustainable Growth Advisers is a global equities firm focused on companies with predictable, above-average long-term earnings and free cash-flow growth.

What We Do

Selective. Diligent. Disciplined.

At SGA, we identify differentiated global businesses that offer predictable and sustainable growth, and invest selectively in those that we believe offer the best investment opportunity over a three- to five-year time horizon.

Our process is designed to increase the odds that companies meet our quality and growth expectations, while managing price risk through disciplined valuation.

Warning

Company Imposters

The investment management industry, including SGA, has seen an increase in reports of fake websites and social media accounts impersonating firms and staff. These fraudsters use firm brands or names of representatives to try to scam and defraud others.

The fraudsters impersonating SGA are not authorized by SGA and SGA is not associated with accounts on social media and similar applications that request direct investments. SGA does not offer direct investment opportunities to individuals through any social media platform.

Please contact us if you suspect someone is impersonating our firm.
