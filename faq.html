Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about SGA's investment strategies, team, and approach to sustainable growth investing.

What is Sustainable Growth Advisers' investment approach?

SGA is a global equity investment manager focused on identifying high-quality companies with sustainable long-term earnings and free cash-flow growth. Our strategies are built on selective company identification, diligent research, and disciplined valuation.

What investment strategies does SGA offer?

SGA offers multiple strategies including International Growth, Global Growth, U.S. Large Cap Growth, Emerging Markets Growth, Global Mid Cap Growth, and Global Focused Growth, each tailored to different market exposures and risk profiles.

How does SGA incorporate ESG factors?

Material ESG factors are integrated into our equity research and portfolio decisions. We believe sustainable business practices can contribute to durable competitive advantages and long-term cash-flow generation.

Who are the key members of the SGA team?

SGA's team includes experienced investment professionals including James Lindsay, David Shapiro, Joseph Wahba, Kishore Rao, Tucker Brown, Peter Knudsen, Anjali Aggarwal, and Alexandra Lee, each bringing deep expertise in global equity research and portfolio management.

What is SGA's fiduciary standard?

As a Virtus Investment Partner, SGA maintains institutional-quality fiduciary standards with a relentless focus on sustainable, long-term earnings and free cash-flow growth for our clients.

Does SGA manage institutional assets?

Yes, SGA manages assets for institutional investors including pension funds, endowments, foundations, and other qualified and sophisticated institutional investors seeking global equity exposure with a sustainable growth focus.
